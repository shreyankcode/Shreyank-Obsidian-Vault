Auto Calculate location notification - The idea is that 2 people will be linked to each other via cloud account. Now Person 1 if reaches at a certain location then a notification will trigger and inform to Person 2.
Example: Person 1 is coming from his home to office. Person 2 stays in the middle but far from office which is not in Person 1’s route, So Person 2 will take metro and reach a common meeting point. But It’s no point in Person 2 to leave his house until Person 1 reaches a certain point in his route, so there is no waiting countlessly for both of them to arrive at a certain meeting point.

Concept
Two people are connected through a shared cloud account or by linking their accounts within the app.

The app automatically monitors the location of Person 1. When Person 1 reaches a predefined location along their journey, the app instantly sends a notification to Person 2.

This eliminates the need for constant messaging or manually sharing live locations.

Example
Person 1 is traveling from home to the office.

Person 2 lives between Person 1's home and office but in a different direction, so Person 1 cannot pick them up directly. Instead, both plan to meet at a common location that Person 2 can reach via metro.

If Person 2 leaves too early, they may end up waiting unnecessarily at the meeting point. If they leave too late, Person 1 may have to wait.

To solve this, Person 2 configures a trigger based on Person 1's journey—for example, "Notify me when Person 1 reaches Location X."

As soon as Person 1 enters the predefined geofence around Location X, Person 2 receives an automatic notification, prompting them to leave for the meeting point.

This ensures both people arrive at approximately the same time, minimizing unnecessary waiting and making coordination effortless.

## Benefits
- No need for continuous live location sharing.
- No repeated "Where are you?" messages.
- Better coordination for meeting at a common location.
- Saves time by reducing unnecessary waiting for either person.
- Fully automatic once the trigger location is configured
